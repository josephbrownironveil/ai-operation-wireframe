    02

    How a lead travels

    No AI talks to another AI directly — they pass work through the inbox, and the hub turns it into records. A Signature Air or DCB lead makes the full trip without Joe touching it:

    STEP 1
    ChatGPT task fires
    Scheduled hunt runs on its own clock, one task per company.
    STEP 2
    Finds & formats leads
    Name, trade, city, phone, why-they-fit, source. Nothing reveals AI involvement.
    STEP 3
    Emails Gmail
    Subject: “LEADS — SIGNATURE AIR — [date]”. The bus carries it.
    STEP 4
    6:15 AM ingest
    The hub parses the email before Joe is awake.
    STEP 5
    GHL contact created
    Deduped, tagged by company + trade, rationale saved as a note.
    STEP 6
    7:15 AM briefing
    One iPhone push reports what landed. Joe just works the list.
